**CONCEPT**

*Meet Through Clouds * is a collaborative educational art project that encourages children to explore the forms, movements, and stories of clouds.  
Through interdisciplinary learning that integrates meteorology, poetry, and visual arts, participants created drawings, installations, and interactive artworks inspired by cloud observation.  

The project aims to foster environmental awareness, scientific curiosity, and collective imagination.
