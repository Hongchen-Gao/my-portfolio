Sky Blind Box

October 1, 2024 · Hongchen Gao

CONCEPT

Sky Blind Box is a collaborative art and education project that invites children to explore natural, ecological, and cultural phenomena in the sky.
Through observing weather patterns and discussing global issues such as climate change and renewable energy, participants designed small “blind boxes” that interpret their visions of the sky.
The project culminated in a student-curated exhibition, transforming scientific curiosity into artistic imagination and collective reflection.
