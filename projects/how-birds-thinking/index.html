How Birds Are Thinking

December 20, 2024 · Hongchen Gao

CONCEPT

This project combines visual arts and animation to explore the speculative inner world of birds—how they perceive, interact with, and remember their surroundings. Inspired by neuroscience and ethology, it invites the viewer to step into an abstracted bird perspective.

VIDEO

ILLUSTRATIONS



CONCLUSION

The project not only visualizes bird cognition but also reflects on human understanding of non-human minds. The animations and artworks were created digitally using Procreate and Adobe After Effects.
