About

Hongchen Gao

Exploring the intersections of art, education, and technology.

Hongchen Gao is a Boston-based visual artist, educator, and curator from China.
She holds a Master’s degree in Fine Arts and is pursuing a Doctorate in Education.
Her work bridges art, education, and technology, exploring how creativity connects people and communities.
Through painting, installation, and participatory projects, she transforms learning and social spaces into platforms for dialogue and shared imagination.
